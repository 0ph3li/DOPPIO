A napoli habit

in napoli, people who had a little luck paid for two coffees and drank one. the second was "suspended": the barista kept the receipt, and when someone poor walked in and asked if there was a sospeso, there was.

we liked it so much we made it the whole menu. every order here is a doppio, every doppio comes with a second cup, and the wall is never empty for long. it's not charity. it's coffee, twice.
