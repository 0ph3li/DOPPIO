(about) the people — both of them

Whytwo?

because there were always two of us. livia pulls the first shot, ottavio pulls the second, and in 2019 neither of us would let the other do both. so we built a whole coffee house around the argument.

(since) how we got here

2019

a bench and two cups

a bench and two cups

one machine on a cart near the duomo and a bench next to it. we sold two coffees for the price of one for a whole month, by accident. nobody complained. the queue got longer.

the second machine

we bought a second machine so we would stop arguing about who pulls the shot. we still argue. now the coffee is just twice as fast.
